# Third-party data notice

This repository does **not** redistribute LUNG1 CT images, tumor segmentations, or clinical tables. Obtain the source collection directly from The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA): **NSCLC-Radiomics (LUNG1), version 4**, DOI `10.7937/K9/TCIA.2015.PF0M9REI`. Follow the TCIA collection license and required data citation.

`data/locked_split_identifiers.csv` contains only public LUNG1 subject identifiers and the locked development role used in the paper. It contains no imaging voxels, survival outcomes, or clinical covariates.
